The collection

Ten tools, one page.

Each one opens in place and runs entirely on your own machine. Pick the job you need doing — nothing here uploads a single byte.

01Video editor A timeline with layered video and audio tracks, trimming, splitting, transforms, text and fades — rendered out to MP4. Open ↗ 02Remove watermark Box the logo or timestamp and get a clean video back. Inpaint, blur, pixelate or crop it away. Open ↗ 03Video to JPG frames Every frame as a numbered JPEG, delivered as a single ZIP. Thirty a second by default. Open ↗ 04Extract audio Save a soundtrack as WAV, MP3 or M4A — or lift the original stream out untouched. Open ↗ 05Mute a video Strip the audio track. The picture is copied bit-for-bit, so there is no quality loss at all. Open ↗ 06Merge audio into video Give a video a new soundtrack, replacing the original or mixed in beneath it. Open ↗ 07Noise cancellation Spectral subtraction that lifts hiss and hum out of audio, or out of a video's soundtrack. Open ↗ 08Merge into one PDF Any number of PDFs, images, Word files and text documents, combined in the order you choose. Open ↗ 09Merge Word documents Join .docx files into one, carrying across images, lists and numbering intact. Open ↗ 10Convert documents PDF to Word, PDF to images or plain text, Word to PDF, images to PDF. Open ↗
